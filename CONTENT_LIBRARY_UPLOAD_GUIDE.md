## 📏 Struktur Spreadsheet

### **Tab 1: Overview**
- All 30 topics + cluster + insights + angles
- **Gunakan buat:** Referensi cepat, planning mana topic next

### **Tab 2: Gaya 1 — Storytelling (40%)**
- Hook starter | Story arc | Visual direction | CTA | Hashtags | Duration
- **Format:** 30-60 second video (IG Reels + TikTok)
- **Tone:** Personal, vulnerable, relatable
- **Goal:** Saves + shares (high engagement)

### **Tab 3: Gaya 2 — Educational (35%)**
- Claim | Framework | Example | CTA | Duration
- **Format:** 45-90 second video + IG Carousel + LinkedIn article
- **Tone:** Expert, actionable, research-backed
- **Goal:** Comments + shares (thought leadership)

### **Tab 4: Gaya 3 — Community (25%)**
- Testimonial hook | Story format | Transformation | Visual | CTA
- **Format:** 30-60 second video + testimony (IG + LinkedIn)
- **Tone:** Warm, celebrating, social proof
- **Goal:** Comments + connection (community building)

## 🎯 Cara Pakai Library di Production

### **Weekly Workflow (3x/minggu)**

**Monday (Storytelling)**
- Buka Tab "Overview" → pilih 1 topic dari Maxwell Laws (Cluster 1)
- Buka Tab "Gaya 1: Storytelling" → cari topic itu
- Copy hook starter + story arc + CTA
- Record video 30-60s (IG Reels format)
- Post ke IG Reels

**Wednesday (Educational)**
- Buka Tab "Overview" → p ilih 1 topic dari Personal Narrative (Cluster 2)
- Buka Tab "Gaya 2: Educational" → cari topic
- Copy framework + example + CTA
- Create 5-7 slide carousel atau artikel LinkedIn
- Post ke IG Carousel + schedule TikTok

**Friday (Community)**
- Buka Tab "Overview" → pilih 1 topic dari Community/Lifestyle (Cluster 3)
- Buka Tab "Gaya 3: Community" → cari topic
- Reach out ke Bunda untuk testimonial
- Record testimonial video 30-60s
- Post ke IG + LinkedIn
