## 🚀 Next: Production Workflow

### Weekly (3x/minggu):

**Monday — Storytelling (IG Reels)**
- Open "Overview" → pick topic (e.g., Law of Lid)
- Open "Gaya 1" → find topic
- Copy: Hook Starter + Story Arc + CTA
- Record: 30-60s video
- Post to IG Reels
- Mark "Posted" di Overview sheet

**Wednesday — Educational (IG Carousel + TikTok)**
- Pick topic (Personal Narrative cluster)
- Open "Gaya 2"
- Copy: Framework/Content + Example
- Create 5-7 slide carousel atau artikel
- Post IG Carousel + schedule TikTok
- Mark "Posted"

**Friday — Community (IG + LinkedIn)**
- Pick topic (Community cluster)
- Open "Gaya 3"
- Reach Bunda untuk testimonial
- Record: 30-60s video
- Post IG + LinkedIn
- Mark "Posted"
